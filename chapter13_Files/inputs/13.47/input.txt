No one becomes a great man without fighting the most difficult battle. The war may 
not always be fought on a battlefield- it can be anything, such as an exam, a
failure, a fear, or any other challenge in our lives. To become greater, you must 
fight bigger battles. History remembers the people based on the size of their battles. 
Therefore, never be afraid of fighting bigger battles. Fight a bigger battle 
with great enthusiasm and with a smiley face.
